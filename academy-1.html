Our Courses

Hands-on training built around the SKAT equipment you actually use — not generic theory.

Insulating Fluids & Transformer Oil Diagnostics

1 day In person, SKAT facility (Volgograd) Lab & substation engineers

A practical, equipment-focused course on testing transformer oil and other insulating fluids — from sampling to interpreting the result.

  • Oil sampling procedure and safety precautions
  • Breakdown voltage testing with SKAT-M100V (IEC 60156 / GOST 6581)
  • Dielectric loss (tan δ) measurement with SKAT-TANGENS-M (IEC 60247)
  • Interpreting results against acceptance standards
  • Hands-on practice and review of real test reports

HV Acceptance & Routine Testing

2 days In person, hands-on with demo equipment Maintenance engineers & service dealers

A practical course on acceptance and routine testing of high-voltage equipment, working directly with SKAT test sets and benches.

  • Scope and standards for HV acceptance testing
  • AC/DC withstand voltage testing with SKAT-70C/70M/70P
  • Working with stationary test benches (SKAT-SVS, SKAT-STEND-50)
  • Safety procedures during high-voltage testing
  • Preparing test protocols and documentation

Product & Sales Expertise for Distributor Managers

3 days In person, SKAT facility, incl. a plant floor tour Distributor company managers

An intensive, hands-on program for distributor sales teams — covering the full SKAT product line, competitive positioning, and tender procedures.

  • Hands-on walkthrough of the full SKAT line: SKAT-M100V, SKAT-TANGENS-M, SKAT-SVS, SKAT-AVN-70, KVC kilovoltmeters
  • Turning a customer's technical requirements into an accurate equipment specification
  • Russian tender procurement rules (Government Decree No. 719, the "second extra bid" rule, import-substitution preferences)
  • Competitive positioning against other test equipment brands
  • Negotiating with technical and procurement stakeholders; ROI/TCO argumentation
