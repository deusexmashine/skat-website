Applications

Delivering confidence across the power industry.

Power Cable Testing

Our AC/DC high-voltage test sets (SKAT-70M, SKAT-70C) are engineered for the diagnostic testing and withstand testing of medium and high-voltage power cables.

Transformer Oil Testing

We offer comprehensive solutions for liquid dielectric analysis, from the SKAT-M100V for breakdown voltage testing to the SKAT-TANGENS-M for precision tan δ measurements.

Substation Diagnostics

Our stationary test benches (SKAT-SVS Series) and test stands (SKAT-STEND-50) are designed for testing insulators, surge arresters, and current transformers.

PPE Testing

Our SKAT-70P attachment and SKAT-SVS systems allow for the systematic testing of dielectric gloves, boots, and live-line tools.
