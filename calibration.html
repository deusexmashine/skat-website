Calibration & Adjustment

Measurement accuracy is the foundation of safe, trustworthy diagnostics

Over time, any measuring instrument's readings can drift from reference values. Regular calibration confirms the accuracy of your SKAT equipment and keeps it metrologically reliable throughout its service life.

How Calibration Works

Four steps — from initial check to a document confirming your instrument's accuracy.

1

Initial Calibration

The instrument's readings are compared against reference values to assess its current condition and identify any deviation.

2

Adjustment (if needed)

If the deviation exceeds the allowed tolerance, the instrument is adjusted to bring it back in line with the reference.

3

Re-Calibration

A follow-up measurement confirms that the deviation has been corrected.

4

Certificate / Report

Results are documented, including the method, reference standards used, and the values obtained.

Two Calibration Options

SKAT Factory Calibration

Checked against the manufacturer's own reference methods — for routine accuracy checks between formal verifications.

Accredited Calibration

Performed by an independent, accredited metrology laboratory — where officially traceable measurement results are required.
