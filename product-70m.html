About the product

The SKAT-70M series are robust, high-voltage test sets designed for AC and DC withstand testing. They generate sinusoidal AC voltage (50 Hz) and DC voltage (negative or positive polarity) for diagnosing insulation in power cables, surge arresters, solid dielectrics, and personal protective equipment. This dual functionality makes it an indispensable tool for utility maintenance crews and testing laboratories.

Engineered for reliability, the system features a dual-block design that separates the control and high-voltage sections, significantly enhancing operational safety. The intuitive control panel, featuring a clear LCD display and a precision rotary encoder, provides operators with complete command over test parameters.

The SKAT-70M's automatic test sequences include programmable voltage rise rates, breakdown detection with automatic shutdown, and a robust earthing mechanism that ensures a safe discharge of capacitive loads after testing.
