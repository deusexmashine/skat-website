About the product

The SKAT-AVN-70 is a professional active high-voltage load designed specifically for the metrological verification and calibration of dielectric testers (such as SKAT-70, AVID-70, and AID-70 series). This high-power load bank allows technicians to perform accurate validation of voltage and current measurements under load conditions.

Its mobile dielectric cabinet design ensures strength and lightness, while the automatic forced air cooling system efficiently removes heat generated at the resistor assemblies. All connections between modules are made with special high-voltage wire with high-temperature silicone insulation.

Troubleshooting & Software updates

  • No power or cooling: Ensure the cooling system power supply is connected and the unit is plugged in.
  • Incorrect load values: Verify high-voltage jumper connections are secure and correctly configured.
  • Overheating: Check the air filter and ensure the ventilation is not blocked.
