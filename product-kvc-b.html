About the product

The KVC B-Series represents a new generation of precision digital kilovoltmeters featuring an oil-insulated resistive high-voltage divider. Designed for high-accuracy measurements of DC voltage and RMS AC voltage at 50 Hz, these instruments serve as a critical standard for calibrating high-voltage test equipment.

With accuracy classes ranging from 0.25% to 1.0%, the KVC B-Series provides reliable and repeatable results. The USB interface allows for easy connection to a PC, enabling real-time oscilloscope functionality, waveform analysis, and data archiving via dedicated software. Built-in memory stores up to 32 oscillograms with a duration of 3 seconds each.

Interpreting test results

For detailed guidance on calibration procedures, measurement uncertainty, and compliance with international standards, refer to your equipment's user manual and the following:

  • IEC 60060 - High-voltage test techniques
  • IEEE Std 4 - Standard for High-Voltage Testing
  • ISO/IEC 17025 - General requirements for the competence of testing and calibration laboratories

Troubleshooting & Software updates

  • No display or power: Check the AC power connection or USB-C external power supply.
  • Incorrect readings: Ensure all connections are secure and the high-voltage divider is properly grounded.
  • USB communication error: Verify the USB cable connection and restart the PC software.
To update the instrument's firmware or to download the latest PC software for waveform analysis and data management, please contact our technical support team.
