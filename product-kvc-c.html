About the product

The KVC C-Series represents the pinnacle of precision digital kilovoltmeters, featuring a frequency-compensated resistive-capacitive divider with air insulation. This advanced design ensures exceptional stability and low ratio error across a wide range of frequencies, from standard 50/60 Hz to 400 Hz, and includes a dedicated mode for ultra-low frequency (VLF) measurements down to 0.01 Hz.

Designed for the most demanding applications in metrology laboratories, calibration facilities, and advanced field diagnostics, the C-Series delivers a class 0.25 accuracy. Its built-in harmonic analysis engine measures total harmonic distortion (THD) for up to 50 harmonics, providing deep insight into the quality of the measured signal.

The intuitive user interface displays multiple parameters simultaneously, including RMS, average, peak, and DC components. With internal storage for 32 oscillograms and a USB interface for real-time PC connectivity, the KVC C-Series is not just a meter—it is a comprehensive measurement and analysis system.

Troubleshooting & Software updates

  • No display or power: Check the AC power connection or USB-C external power supply.
  • Incorrect readings: Ensure all connections are secure and the high-voltage divider is properly grounded.
  • USB communication error: Verify the USB cable connection and restart the PC software.

To update the instrument's firmware or to download the latest PC software for waveform analysis and data management, please contact our technical support team.
