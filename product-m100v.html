About the product

The SKAT-M100V is a dedicated, fully automatic test set designed to measure the dielectric breakdown voltage of transformer oil and other liquid insulants. The unit strictly follows the requirements of IEC 60156 (GOST 6581-75), providing a reliable and standardized method for assessing oil quality.

The instrument features a fully automated test sequence, a built-in thermal printer for immediate documentation, and a safety door with an interlock system that blocks high voltage application when the lid is open. Operators can select voltage rise rates from 0.5 to 5 kV/s to match specific standards.

For advanced data management, the SKAT-M100V can be connected to external PC software, allowing for detailed analysis and archiving of test results.

FAQ

It measures the dielectric breakdown voltage of transformer oil and other liquid insulants, which indicates the oil's ability to withstand electrical stress.
Yes. The instrument is fully compliant with IEC 60156 and GOST 6581-75, which are the primary standards for testing the dielectric strength of insulating liquids.
The breakdown voltage indicates the presence of contaminants, moisture, or particles in the oil. Low breakdown values can lead to transformer failure.

Interpreting test results

International standards specify interpretation of results and recommended action. For detailed guidance, refer to:

  • IEC 60156
  • IEC 60422
  • IEEE C57.106

Troubleshooting & Software updates

  • Inconsistent results: Ensure the oil sample is clean and the test vessel is properly filled.
