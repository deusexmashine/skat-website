About the product

The SKAT-STEND-50 is a versatile test stand designed specifically for the high-voltage testing of insulating rods, live-line tools, and HV indicators used in substations and transmission lines up to 50 kV.

Its modular design allows for easy assembly and disassembly. The stand supports segmented testing, enabling you to test one object in up to 5 segments or two objects simultaneously in up to 2 segments each. The kit includes dielectric bases for safe object placement, a full set of high-voltage and grounding wires, and 15 temporary electrodes.
